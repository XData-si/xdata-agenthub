# DevOps deployment review

1. Confirm environment, owner, change window and rollback path.
2. Check image provenance, pinned versions, secrets handling and least privilege.
3. Verify health checks, monitoring, backups and restore test evidence.
4. Review network exposure, TLS, access controls and data retention.
5. Confirm staged validation and human approval before production deployment.
6. Record change outcome, incidents and rollback status.

**Output:** go/no-go checklist with evidence and open risks.
